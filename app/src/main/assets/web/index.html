Clean RAM now
Stops background apps when the phone feels slow. Not a battery saver: apps restarted later cost more power than cached ones. Safe: cached processes only. About

VNM Optimizer v2.0.1

Heat & battery tuning, device tools, ADB & Fastboot in one app.

Heat & battery fix for Android. Kernel features support Qualcomm and MediaTek kernels; features a phone does not support are skipped and shown as Unsupported. Tuned and tested on HyperOS. One-shot at boot, no background service (except the opt-in charge-limit and app-cache keepers). Every feature has its own switch and shows exactly what it changes.
